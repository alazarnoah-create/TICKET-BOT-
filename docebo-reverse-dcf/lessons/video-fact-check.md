# Video fact-check: Docebo (DCBO) YouTube overview

Every number the video mentions, checked against what we have from Docebo's own disclosures.

**Key:**
- ✅ **Matches:** agrees with a Docebo filing or release we have.
- ⚠️ **Unverified:** plausible, but we don't have the filing yet.
- ❌ **Wrong or out of date:** contradicts a reported number.

**Lesson:** a YouTube video is a starting point, not a source. One of its money figures was badly wrong (the 365Talents price), and several are undated. Always trace a number back to the company's own filing.
