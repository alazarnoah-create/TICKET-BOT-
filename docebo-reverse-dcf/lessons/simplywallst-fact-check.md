# Simply Wall St fact-check: Docebo (TSX: DCBO)

**Source:** the live Simply Wall St pages Noah pasted on 30 Sep 2026. They say "Company Analysis updated 2026/09/29; end-of-day price 2026/09/28". Their data comes from S&P Global.

**How it was checked:** an independent verification run (researchers plus skeptics) compared each claim with Docebo's own releases and filings through search results, because the filing sites are blocked from this environment.

**Key:**
- ✅ **Matches:** agrees with Docebo's releases or filings.
- ⚠️ **Third-party only:** analyst, market or estimate data that Docebo doesn't report.
- ❌ **Wrong:** contradicts Docebo's own documents.

> **Correction:** an earlier version of this file said Simply Wall St shows Docebo as "debt free, 6/6 health checks". That came from an **old snapshot in search results**, not the live page. The live page shows debt of US$87.95M, negative equity and **2/6** health checks.

## Lessons
1. **Always check the date of third-party data.** An old search snapshot said "debt free". The live page and Docebo's filings say US$88M of debt.
2. **Look for one-offs.** 2025 profit looked great partly because of a US$13.1M tax gain.
3. **Watch buyback prices.** Buying back shares at US$55 and US$29 that now trade near US$24 destroyed value for the shareholders who didn't sell.
4. **Negative equity breaks ratios.** Debt-to-equity and return on equity stop meaning anything.
