TICKET BOT FOR MAC

1. Drag "Ticket Bot" onto the Applications folder.
2. Open Ticket Bot from Applications.

"Ticket Bot" Not Opened?
This app isn't from the App Store, so macOS blocks it the first time:
  - Click Done.
  - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
    "Open Anyway" next to the Ticket Bot message. Enter your password.
  - Open Ticket Bot again and click Open.
You only have to do this once.

Or skip the warning completely: paste this into Terminal instead of using this disk image:
  curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alazarnoah-create/TICKET-BOT-/HEAD/install.sh | bash

Ticket Bot needs Python 3. If it's missing, the app tells you and takes you to python.org.
