How it works

  1. Every morning at 9:55 AM ET, Claude looks up the day's games: starting pitchers, team stats, betting odds, line movement and news.
  2. The model predicts runs for each team from its hitting, the other team's starting pitcher and bullpen, and the ballpark.
  3. It plays out every possible final score to get the chance that each bet wins.
  4. It compares that chance with the sportsbook's odds. "Value" means the model thinks the bet wins more often than the price says.
  5. This page updates by itself and your phone gets a notification with the top picks.
